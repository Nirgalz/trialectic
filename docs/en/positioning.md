# Positioning of the Trialectic Method

> **Status: proposed positioning statement, not an approved normative definition.** This document describes the Method's purpose without changing its concepts or rules.
>
> **Languages:** [English](positioning.md) · [Français](../fr/positionnement.md)
>
> **Conceptual reference:** [Trialectic Method](../../reference/trialectic-method.md)

## Concise definition

> The Trialectic Method is a framework for structuring and critically examining reasoning about possibilities for action, the conditions of their Actualization, and their transformations. It aims to make such reasoning explicit, comparable, and revisable, individually or collectively, including with the assistance of artificial intelligence.

## Purpose and scope

This positioning emphasizes a methodological function: clarifying, examining, comparing, and revising reasoning about complex situations.

The Method can support the recursive phases **Understand → Explore → Decide**. An analysis may stop after understanding or exploration if that meets its objective. It examines Subjects situated in their Environment, may consider relevant meanings and representations, and uses NEEDs, CANs, conditions of Actualization, and Scenarios when useful.

AI assistance, including trIA, may help represent and question these analyses. It does not remove the need to verify sources, assumptions, relationships, and inferences.

## Limits of the claims

- This positioning **does not present** the Method as a universal ontology or a theory explaining every phenomenon.
- It **does not claim** to have invented each concept or cognitive process it draws on.
- It **does not establish** superiority over existing approaches. Its pedagogical and analytical benefits remain to be evaluated empirically.
- Exploratory Scenarios are not guaranteed predictions, and a structured representation is not proof.
